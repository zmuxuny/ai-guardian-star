# Code of Conduct · 行为守则

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is authoritative; the summary below is provided for convenience.

本项目采用 [Contributor Covenant 行为守则 2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。以官方全文为准，下方摘要仅供参考。

---

## English

### Our standards

Everyone taking part in this project — contributors, maintainers, and anyone opening an Issue or pull request — is expected to:

- treat others with respect, and refrain from personal attacks, discrimination, or harassment;
- avoid offensive or insulting language, and respect differing viewpoints and cultural backgrounds;
- keep discussion focused on the facts and the technical problem.

### Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the maintainers with supporting evidence such as links or screenshots. Use a private channel — a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/zmuxuny/ai-guardian-star/security/advisories) draft works for confidential reports — rather than a public Issue.

Maintainers will investigate and may take action, including a warning, removal of content, or a ban from participation. Reporter identities are kept confidential.

---

## 中文

### 行为规范

所有参与者（包括贡献者、维护者、提交 Issue 或 PR 的人）都应做到：

- 尊重他人，不发表人身攻击、歧视或骚扰性言论；
- 避免使用攻击性或侮辱性语言，尊重不同观点与文化差异；
- 讨论中关注事实和问题本身。

### 举报与处理

若你遭遇或目睹任何不当行为，请向维护者举报并提供相关证据（例如链接、截图）。请使用私下渠道，涉及隐私的内容可通过 [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/zmuxuny/ai-guardian-star/security/advisories) 草稿提交，不要发在公开 Issue。

维护者将进行调查，并在必要时采取警告、移除内容或禁止参与等措施。举报者身份将被保密。
